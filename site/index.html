II · Architecture: Blind to the Athlete, Legible to the Judge

Design Doctrine · Metrology Over Optics

Editorial illustration. Like Olympic timing, the athlete sees zero table fiducials or judge readouts; instruments outside the workspace measure mass, depth, and spatial position.

01 · Unpredictable Sealed Draw

Future randomness prevents pre-computed memorizing

Public future-round drand-quicknet randomness draws task parameters right before execution. Every attempt registers in an append-only SlotLedger before running, blocking cherry-picking and deleted retries.

02 · Early-Human Body, Zero Table Crutches

Strong vision, opposable thumbs, plain language

Version one mandates two arms, 5-finger opposable-thumb hands, fixed stereo RGB cameras, and natural text commands on an unassisted table. Runs over inspect-robots==1.0.0.

03 · Hidden Instruments + Offline Certificate

Auditable custody from raw wire bytes to final score

Settled 21-byte RS232 balance frames, socket depth switches, and sub-millimeter overhead homography grade physical state directly, sealed with Ed25519 DSSE signatures and FreeTSA RFC 3161 timestamps.

IV · Two Certification Tracks, One Custody Layer

Open Kit · Track B

Public Benchmark & Reproducible Hardware Standard

  • Open bimanual rig bill of materials (P1 from ~$26.5k, P2 tactile from ~$40.9k, Stage-1 validation sensor bench $1,291) with fixed head stereo RGB and natural text input.
  • Every published score pairs a human teleoperation body ceiling, a scripted floor, and full registered attempt denominators.
  • Full public artifact release: target geometry, raw balance/camera logs, seed_reveal.json, and offline verification bundles.
Closed Bank · Track C

Independent Capability & Hazard-Uplift Certification

  • Open harness, open sensor drivers, and open scoring rules paired with a private task bank and benign-surrogate hazardous-skill tasks (dyed water, harmless tracers, sealed containers).
  • Public auditors verify Ed25519 signatures, pre-beacon drand + FreeTSA chronology, and attempt counts while keeping private task layouts concealed.
  • Authorized escrow auditors verify full deterministic seed-to-target derivation and re-score raw instrument recordings.
